# Announcing: Code & Canvas

I'm thrilled to announce my debut book, *Code & Canvas: Where Engineering Meets Art*.

## What's It About?

After years of working as a software engineer while maintaining creative side projects, I've developed a philosophy about the relationship between technical and artistic work. This book is my attempt to articulate that philosophy.

## Key Themes

### The False Dichotomy

We're taught that "left brain" and "right brain" people are fundamentally different. I argue this is not only false but actively harmful to both engineers and artists.

### Code as Medium

Just as a painter chooses oils or watercolors, developers choose languages and frameworks. These choices are creative decisions that shape the final work.

### Constraints as Freedom

The best creative work often comes from tight constraints. The same is true in engineering. This section explores how to use limitations as springboards for innovation.

### Building a Creative Practice

Practical advice for maintaining creativity alongside demanding technical work.

## Who Is This For?

- Engineers who miss making art
- Artists curious about technology
- Anyone who's felt caught between "creative" and "technical" identities

## Availability

*Code & Canvas* is available now in digital and print formats.

I hope this book helps bridge a divide that I believe shouldn't exist.
