# Severance: A Masterclass in Corporate Horror

Apple TV+ has produced many quality shows, but *Severance* stands apart as something truly special. Created by Dan Erickson and directed by Ben Stiller, this show takes the concept of work-life balance and pushes it to its most extreme conclusion.

## The Premise

Employees at Lumon Industries undergo a "severance" procedure that surgically divides their memories between work and personal life. Your "innie" exists only at work, while your "outie" remembers nothing of what happens there.

## What Makes It Brilliant

The show works on multiple levels:

- **As corporate satire**: The absurdist office culture, complete with "melon bars" and "waffle parties" as rewards, skewers modern workplace wellness culture
- **As psychological thriller**: The mystery of what Lumon actually does keeps you guessing
- **As existential drama**: Questions about identity, consciousness, and what makes us *us*

## The Cast

Adam Scott delivers a career-best performance as Mark S., playing both versions of the character with subtle distinctions. Patricia Arquette is deliciously sinister as the department head who truly believes in the company mission.

## Verdict

*Severance* is the rare show that gets better with every episode. The season one finale is one of the most tense, emotionally satisfying hours of television I've ever seen.

**Must watch.** Clear your schedule.
