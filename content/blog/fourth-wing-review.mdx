# Fourth Wing: Dragon Fantasy Done Right

Rebecca Yarros' *Fourth Wing* took BookTok by storm, and for once, the hype is justified. This is comfort reading elevated to an art form.

## The Setup

Violet Sorrengail was supposed to become a scribe. Instead, her general mother forces her into Basgiath War College to become a dragon rider—a position where the weak don't just fail, they die.

## What Works

**The world-building** is surprisingly robust for a romantasy. The magic system based on dragon bonds, the political intrigue, the hints of a larger war—it all clicks together.

**The pacing** is relentless. Yarros knows exactly when to throw another obstacle at Violet, whether it's assassination attempts, demanding professors, or complicated feelings for her enemies.

**The dragons** have genuine personality. Tairn, Violet's dragon, steals every scene he's in.

## What Doesn't Quite Land

Some of the side characters feel underdeveloped, and the "surprise" villain is telegraphed early. But these are minor quibbles.

## The Romance

Yes, it's spicy. Yes, it's enemies-to-lovers. Yes, I read it in one sitting. No further questions.

## Bottom Line

*Fourth Wing* knows exactly what it is and executes it perfectly. If you're looking for dragons, danger, and a healthy dose of drama, this delivers.
